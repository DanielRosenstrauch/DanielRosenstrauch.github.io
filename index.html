H.E.A.T.: localized textile bonding for soft robotics

The Preston Innovation Lab bonds textiles into inflatable wearables with a full-surface heat press, which wastes material and limits geometry. We turned a Prusa MINI 3D printer into a bonding tool that traces heat and pressure along a toolpath, with magnets holding the fabric in place.

Stress analysis · Spring 2026 · With Hayden Webb

Eclipse: a pressure vessel optimizer for a hybrid rocket

The gas stage of a two-stage hybrid rocket needs a thick-walled pressure vessel that gets it to 30,000 ft. Our model finds the maximum safe pressure from Lamé stresses and the von Mises criterion, integrates drag work through a standard atmosphere, and searches 256,000 geometries across four materials and three fuels for the best trade between mass and cost.

The class project was written in MATLAB. I rebuilt it in Python as an interactive web tool, with tests that check it against the original report. The full sweep of about 3 million cases runs in under a second.

iSEED Tokyo · May to June 2025 · Team of 3

Curb-O: a robot that takes the trash to the curb

An autonomous platform that carries a trash bin to the curb and back, for elderly and disabled users who can’t haul a full one. It follows a taped lane with two color sensors, stops for obstacles with two ultrasonic sensors, and starts when a homemade limit switch detects the bin. A pulley with a quick-release pin lifts bags into the bin.

We had one week to build it. Every subsystem passed on its own; the full assembly was too heavy for the motors, and tracing that failure taught me to do the power budget first.

Research · Aug 2023 to May 2024 · Phillips Academy Andover

Stellar spectrograph

I built an f/5.6 spectrograph that measures the spectra of dim stars, on a $200 budget, for the school’s astronomy department to use in research on the chemical composition of stars.

Light enters through a 10 micron slit, a collimator lens makes it parallel, a diffraction grating spreads it by wavelength, and a second lens focuses the spectrum onto the camera.
